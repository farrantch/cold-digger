# Code of conduct

Cold Digger contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful,
welcoming, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of another
person's sensitive data are not acceptable. This is especially important for a
forensics project: never post real case material, credentials, private keys, or
personal information in issues, discussions, or pull requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed
to protect contributors and users. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainer through the contact options on their GitHub profile.
